About Highway Vocational Center

A NITA registered institution committed to transforming lives through quality, practical vocational training.

Who We Are

Highway Vocational Center is a NITA registered institution committed to transforming lives by offering inclusive training opportunities that build skills, foster self-reliance, and promote economic development.

As a Center of Excellence, we provide quality training in Beauty and Therapy, HairDressing, Computer Packages, and Cyber & eCitizen Services.

Located in Runyenjes, Embu County, we serve students from across Kenya who are seeking practical, market-relevant skills.

Accreditation

Highway Vocational Center is fully registered and accredited by the National Industrial Training Authority (NITA), Kenya.

NITA Registered TVET Compliant Quality Assured

Report Back to School

Confirm your return for the new term. Any overpayment credit from a previous term will be automatically deducted from your current balance.

How Report Back Works
  • If you have an overpayment credit from a previous term, the system automatically applies it to your current term fees — regardless of the amount.
  • If the credit covers your full balance, your balance becomes KES 0 and the remaining credit stays as a carry-forward.
  • If you have no overpayment, the system shows you the exact balance you must pay for the term you are reporting into.
  • Every report back is recorded on your ledger and visible to administration.

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

How we collect, use, and protect your personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Data We Collect

We collect your name, contact details, academic records, identification documents, and payment records for the purpose of processing your application and administering your training.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for admission processing, academic administration, fee management, and official communication. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact the administration office to exercise these rights.

4. Data Retention

We retain student records for the duration required by TVET and NITA regulations, and thereafter securely dispose of them.

5. Contact

For data protection inquiries, contact highwayvocational@gmail.com or call 0722 650 030.
